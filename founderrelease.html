From assumptions
to evidence.

An application can run without answering every question about how it behaves. Our development work focuses on making those questions and the supporting evidence easier to review.

Define the scope.

Identify the software version, workflow, and requirements being considered.

Prepare the evidence.

Organize supplied records and checks so missing information and coverage gaps can be seen.

Keep decisions accountable.

Develop a review approach that makes uncertainty visible and keeps release authority with the responsible person.

These describe the project’s direction. Preparing evidence does not by itself establish that software is safe, verified, or authorized for release.
